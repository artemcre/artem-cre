Creation

01 — what we make

Give what you see a form.

Poetry, photography, spoken word, drawing, painting, writing, and other creative works are ways of taking something within us and giving it a form that someone else can encounter.

Here, a creation is not only something to look at. It is an invitation to see what another person chose to express, and to discover what it becomes through your own perspective.

Empathy

03 — what we understand

Make room for another perspective.

Psychology and mental health are ways of learning about the mind, the experiences we carry, and the struggles that can be difficult to see from the outside.

Empathy begins with listening. Here, learning is not only about knowing more. It is about becoming more willing to understand experiences that may not look or feel like our own.

About ARTEM

05 — the story behind the space

A space built around perspective.

ARTEM is a space for creative expression, reflection, and mental health education. It brings together things people make, things people think about, and things people are trying to understand.

At its heart, ARTEM is about making room for different ways of seeing and for people to be both heard and understood.
